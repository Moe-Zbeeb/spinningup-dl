Open with one short paragraph that welcomes the reader and says what this part is about. It sits under the table of contents, and under the "Coming soon" box for as long as the part has unwritten sections. Then list what the part covers, taken from its section headings, as Spinning Up does. In this part, we aim to acquaint you with

- the first idea, in a few words,
- the second idea,
- and the third idea.

When the notes or the maintainer say what the reader should already know, close the opening with one sentence that says so and links to the page that teaches it. Otherwise leave that sentence out.

## First Topic

Every topic is one `##` section, written in the shape shown in `templates/section.md`. Section headings are short labels in Title Case, without numbers.

## Recap

A finished part ends with a recap: two or three sentences on what the reader can now do, and a link to where they should go next. The recap is added, and the "Coming soon" box removed, when the last section is written.
