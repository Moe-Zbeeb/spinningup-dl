# About AUB–FAF

Spinning Up in Deep Learning is written and maintained by [AUB Frontier AI Fellows](https://aub-faf.com/) (AUB–FAF), a credit-bearing research program within the Vertically Integrated Projects Program (VIP) at the Maroun Semaan Faculty of Engineering and Architecture (MSFEA), American University of Beirut.

The program brings undergraduates into small research teams. Each team has an AUB faculty supervisor, a Lebanese AI mentor from a frontier lab, university, or company, and one or two undergraduate students. Students learn the habits behind serious AI research: reading papers, reproducing results, implementing ideas, running experiments, and communicating what they find.

To learn more about the program, see previous work, or express interest, visit [aub-faf.com](https://aub-faf.com/).
