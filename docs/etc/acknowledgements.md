## Spinning Up in Deep RL

The idea for this site comes from [Spinning Up in Deep RL](https://spinningup.openai.com/), an educational resource on reinforcement learning written by Joshua Achiam at OpenAI and released under the MIT License. Spinning Up in Deep Learning borrows its name, its structure, its teaching approach, and its design. If you want to learn reinforcement learning, read the original.

## The Deep Learning Specialization

The course follows the topics of the [Deep Learning Specialization](https://www.deeplearning.ai/courses/deep-learning-specialization/) by Andrew Ng, offered by DeepLearning.AI on Coursera, whose five courses map onto the five parts of our [Introduction to Deep Learning](../intro/neural_networks.md).

## Course Notes by Amir Masoud Sefidian

Parts [2](../intro/improving_networks.md), [3](../intro/structuring_projects.md), [4](../intro/convnets.md), and [5](../intro/sequence_models.md) follow the outlines of Amir Masoud Sefidian's notes on the specialization:

- [Improving Deep Neural Networks](https://www.sefidian.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Improving-Deep-Neural-Networks.pdf), 2022,
- [Structuring Machine Learning Projects](https://www.sefidian.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Structuring-Machine-Learning-Projects.pdf), 2023,
- [Convolutional Neural Networks](https://www.sefidian.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Convolutional-Neural-Networks-Notes.pdf), 2022,
- and [Sequence Models](https://www.sefidian.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Sequence-Models.pdf), 2023.

The explanations, examples, figures, and code on this site are our own.
